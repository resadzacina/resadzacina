About me

I'm Resad Zacina - founder, investor, and operator obsessed with turning ideas into real businesses. Over the last decade I've helped launch 115+ products, grown Ministry of Programming into a Deloitte Fast 50 and FT1000 company, and worked across Europe, the Nordics, and MENA with founders who move fast and think big.

I've been part of standout European successes like Fishbrain, Stylemarks/Juniqe, and NAGA, and I've collaborated with enterprises such as Roche, Triton Digital, and Raiffeisen Bank on software used by millions.

At MOP, we've built a portfolio of startups that now spans AI, deep tech, health, fintech, mobility, creator economy, and more.

These days, I split my time between building AI-driven products, investing in early-stage teams, and helping founders sharpen their strategy, product, and growth. I love working with people who have intensity, obsession, and ambition — the ones who actually ship.
